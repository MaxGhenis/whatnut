## 9. What `whatnut.results` exposes

Module-level names: `DEFAULT_PATH`, `ROOT`, `Results`, `ResultsError`, `SEXES`, `r`.

`Results` members (`r = whatnut.results.r`):

- `__getitem__`: method (name: 'str') -> 'Any'
- `bracket`: method (sex: 'str', member: 'str', stat: 'str' = 'mean') -> 'float' — The reference case (years).
- `data`: cached property
- `days_per_year`: property
- `le_gain`: method (sex: 'str', a0: 'int', delta: 'float', background: 'float', member: 'str', stat: 'str' = 'mean') -> 'float' — Change in remaining life expectancy (years) at age ``a0`` from moving
- `le_gain_days`: method (*args, **kwargs) -> 'float'
- `marginal`: method (sex: 'str', background: 'float', member: 'str', stat: 'str' = 'mean') -> 'float' — Years gained from the next ``marginal_step_g`` grams at the reference age.
- `members`: property
- `meta`: property
- `reference`: property
- `stats`: property
- `verify`: method () -> 'None' — Re-derive headline numbers from the grid; raise ResultsError on mismatch.
- `path`: dataclass field (default `results/results.json`, or `$WHATNUT_RESULTS`)

Grid axes (`meta.grid_axes`): {"a0": [30, 40, 50, 60, 70], "background": [0, 10, 20, 28], "delta": [5, 10, 15, 20, 28, 40], "member": ["floor_low", "floor_high", "cvd_only", "calibrated_all_cause", "calibrated_overall", "calibrated", "face_value", "c_0.1", "c_0.33", "c_1"], "sex": ["female", "male"], "stat": ["mean", "p10", "p90", "p2_5", "p97_5"]}

Sections without a typed accessor (read with `r['<section>']`): `ala`, `baseline_2021`, `baseline_le`, `c_dial`, `calibration`, `cost`, `curves`, `fadnes`, `figures`, `phase_in`. Typed accessors cover `grid` (`le_gain`, `le_gain_days`), `reference` (`reference`, `bracket`), `marginal_10g` (`marginal`) and `meta` (`meta`, `days_per_year`, `members`, `stats`).

Paper placeholders defined today in `paper/fill_paper.py`: `{{mc_draws}}`, `{{ref_male_calibrated_mean_days}}`; tables: `{{table:reference_bracket}}`. Formatters: `number`, `integer`, `days`, `years`, `dollars`, `pct`, `ratio`, `text`, `interval`, `md_table`.
