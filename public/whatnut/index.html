Working paper

What nut?

How much remaining life a US adult gains by eating nuts every day, by age, sex and amount; how much the next grams add given what they already eat; and whether the kind of nut matters once price counts. For a 40-year-old man who eats none, 15 grams a day adds 6 to 22 days through the LDL pathway alone, 470 after discounting the cohort association by the typical gap between nutrition trials and cohorts, and 724 if the association is causal. The evidence cannot rank the tree nuts, and macadamias cost 6 times as much per gram as peanuts. This page embeds revision 1 of the manuscript.
